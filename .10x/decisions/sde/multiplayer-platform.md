### Deviations

1. **Origin allowlist built** although the ADR amendment and R-2 said none: requested in this step's prompt (newer than both). ADR amendment annotated.
2. **Per-IP connection cap is not possible on Workers**; built a per-address **request rate** (rate-limit binding, per location, approximate, documented as a backstop) and keyed the hard limits per account inside the Durable Objects. The WAF rule stays a user action (U-8d).
3. **Producer is an outbox entry**, created in `endOfHand` and sent after `record_hand`, rather than a direct `HAND_QUEUE.send` in `endOfHand`: the consumer never races the archive, and a send failure is retried durably.
4. `verify_hand`/`record_incident`/`audit_hand` are **security invoker** (not definer like `record_hand`): the service role needs nothing more.
5. Scripts are TypeScript run by Node 22's type stripping (`node scripts/x.ts`); the smoke is an `.mjs` that bundles its TS runner with esbuild, because engine imports have no extensions.
6. `REPORT_CONTACT` is `null`: the Fair play page shows interim wording until the owner picks an address (U-6).
7. Tests changed by intended behaviour (all tightened or extended, none loosened): oversized frame now must close 4400; the forfeit test's "nothing left to fire" became "only the cleanup deadline is left, and it deletes the table"; outbox key lists gain `:verify` entries; the secret-key destination test allows the three new service functions; migration/function lists gain the new migration and functions; the leak allowlist gains `own`.

### Tech debt

- Lobby `presence` is still one broadcast per accepted frame or connect: one account can push at most 4 per second (budget refill) to everyone in the lobby. Coalescing presence would remove it.
- A replaced tab learns it was replaced only when its browser times out the close handshake (~2 s in Chromium); its clicks in that window are dropped, never applied.
- `outbox_retry` attempts keep growing while play continues during an outage (each hand end retries at once), so after a long outage on a finished table the next retry can be 5 minutes away.
- `hands.created_at` has no index; `/api/stats` does 14 head counts per 5 minutes per isolate. Fine at launch volumes; the DBA review should decide.
- Actions run on Node 20 under the forced Node 24 runtime (GitHub's deprecation warning): bump the pinned majors in a separate change.
- SR-10 (dev ids may be UUID-shaped) stays open: the chaos pass relies on UUID dev accounts to archive locally.
- `npm audit` dev-only highs (SR-12) unchanged.

### Deviations

1. The present player is not put at the head of the queue after a no-show; "Find another match" re-queues them with a fresh `since`. Simpler, and the queue is short at launch.
2. The one-table rule asks the table (`liveness`) rather than trusting `active:`; a release lost to an error never locks anyone out. Release is a direct RPC, not through the outbox.
3. `/api/me` returns only `activeMatch` (no profile yet).
4. The client bail-out shows after 60 s when the server says one person is queued, as the ADR says; presence counts accounts with a lobby socket open.
5. e2e uses per-run player names: the local server keeps state between runs, and an unfinished table correctly keeps an account busy.

### Tech debt

- Pair limiter keys use the UTC day; a pair can meet 2× before and 2× after midnight UTC.
- No rate limit on lobby frames yet (the table's token bucket is also still pending, Step 7).

### Deviations from the ADR (all deliberate)

1. **`SeatView` gains `actions: HandAction[]` and `lastRaise: number`.** Both are public (derivable from the betting). `toHeroGame` needs them to rebuild `Game.history` and `Game.lastRaise`, which `Table`, `spotKey`/`rangeWeights` and `heroContext → legalActions` read. The redaction allowlist includes them.
2. **`act` uses a structural clone, not `structuredClone`.** Config, deck, recorded actions and hole-card tuples are never mutated after creation, so they are shared; players, pots and the board are copied. Measured 9× faster (≈ 30 µs → 3.4 µs per HU action) and the invariant suite went from 92 s to 26 s. Immutability is unchanged and asserted by the illegal-action stream (input JSON identical after a rejected call).
3. **A fold removes the seat from earlier streets' pots immediately.** The ADR rebuilt pots only at round close, which left a folded seat "eligible" mid-street. The invariant suite caught this on its first run.
4. **Folding is always legal**, even when checking is free, as in `poker.ts` (the differential requires it).
5. **Short raises.** The ADR allowed a below-minimum raise only as the raiser's all-in. The engine also allows raising to `maxRaiseTo` when that cap comes from the deepest opponent's stack, exactly as `poker.ts` does heads-up (required for the differential). Such a raise never reopens the betting (it does not bump `raiseSeq`), and since it puts the deepest opponent all-in nobody behind can re-raise anyway.
6. **After settlement `pots` are kept** for the award animation; `assertInvariants` then checks `Σ stack === chips`, `Σ awards === Σ pots` and `Σ net === 0` instead of the live-hand equation.
7. **`Date.now` is lint-banned in the engine and worker too**, not only `Math.random`/timers: the DO must take its clock from one place so deadlines are testable.
8. **`src/test/setup.ts` is DOM-guarded** so node-environment suites can share it.
