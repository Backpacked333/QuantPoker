## Findings

1. **Exploit (fixed):** moves the clock made were not graded. A TAG script that left its folds to the clock scored 87.0 instead of 79.4, with identical play. Every move is now graded.
2. **Weakness (documented):** the plain per-decision mean ignores stakes.
   - Pot-raise-every-street scores 72.0 and min-raise 70.6, against Atlas's 74.9, while giving up 4 to 6 times Atlas's EV per decision.
   - Each pot-weighted alternative breaks the required order and is noisier, so the plain mean stays.
   - Each decision's pot is now stored, so the real-data Spearman analysis can compare variants.
3. **Defence in depth (fixed, from review):** `record_grades` now refuses unverified and casual hands.
